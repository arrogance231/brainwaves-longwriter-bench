Continuity task: Arro, Minami, Akari, and Shiori are in a school setting. Arro keeps trying to help Minami find a girlfriend while avoiding his own feelings for Akari. Shiori knows about Arro's hesitation; Akari has noticed it too but hasn't confronted him directly. The group dynamic remains unresolved with Arro deflecting through practical suggestions.

The vending machine hummed behind them as the group stood clustered near the stairwell door. Rain had stopped, leaving puddles that reflected the pale afternoon light filtering through the high windows.

"So what do we do?" Minami asked, kicking at a scuffed floor tile. "The practical's not going to arrange itself."

Arro opened his mouth to suggest they split up and talk to different people—then caught himself. That was what he'd been doing for weeks. Breaking problems into manageable pieces so he never had to look at the whole thing.

"We could just... go to the cultural festival together," Akari said quietly. She was looking at her hands, fingers worrying at the hem of her sleeve. "All four of us. See what happens."

Shiori's eyes flicked between Arro and Akari. Her expression didn't change, but something shifted in the way she stood—shoulders squared, ready.

"That's actually not terrible," Arro said quickly. Too quickly. He cleared his throat. "I mean, logistically it makes sense. We're already paired up for the practical, so spending time together would—"

"Would what?" Shiori cut in gently.

He stopped talking. Looked at the rain-darkened window instead of either of them.

Minami sighed and ran a hand through his hair. "You guys are impossible. Fine. Cultural festival. But if anyone tries to play matchmaker I'm leaving."

Akari smiled faintly. It was small, careful, like she was testing whether it might break.

Arro felt his chest tighten. He nodded once, hard, and turned toward the door before anyone could see what that smile did to him.
